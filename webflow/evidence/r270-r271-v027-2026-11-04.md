# R-270 menu timing and R-271 resize fix — 4 November 2026

## Menu trigger timing (R-270)

Read-only browser inspection of the published staging sites found a trigger/panel timing mismatch on both installed SmashBurger instances. No Webflow styles or page content were changed.

**Voice Focus:** At a 511 px viewport, the trigger is 56 × 56 px, plum with soft-pelican text, and transitions `background-color` over 180 ms. While opening, the panel reports 280 ms for opacity and transform. This reproduces the reported 100 ms difference: the trigger colour finishes first. The navbar's own configured close duration reads 220 ms, so the panel's measured 280 ms comes from the rendered page styles.

**madewithpixels:** At a 2044 px viewport, the trigger is 72 × 72 px. Its base background is Coral; hover, focus and open styling use Raspberry. Its computed transition duration is 0 s, while the configured panel close duration reads 220 ms. This is a more abrupt colour change than the Voice Focus fade. No state-specific size change was found in the inspected trigger rules.

On madewithpixels, the menu was closed by 40 ms after a trigger click at the wide viewport. This is consistent with the reported resize bug, but the resize event itself was not captured in this pass. The MWP page was left in its closed state. A full width/state matrix and visual inspection of every close frame remain open; no design adjustment was made.

## Resize fix and release (R-271)

The v0.2.6 handler now returns immediately when a non-initial resize leaves the navbar on the same collapsed/expanded breakpoint. A real breakpoint change still runs the existing close/expand behavior. Regression tests cover both cases.

## Evidence limits

The timing measurements are rendered browser observations at the stated widths, not proof at every breakpoint. The MWP early close was observed, but its event source was not instrumented. Local tests verify the fix's same-breakpoint no-op and breakpoint-change behavior; they do not replace acceptance on the published consumer pages.
